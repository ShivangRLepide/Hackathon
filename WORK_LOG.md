## Decisions and known gaps
- Overlap priority: card > NINO > phone > email (risk-descending). Matches claimed greedily in that order, so one span is never counted twice.
- Phone: national first digit must be 7 (mobile), or 1/2/3 (geographic); other values are rejected.
- Masking: card shows last 4 digits; NINO shows prefix and suffix letter; phone shows first 2 and last 3 digits of the 11-digit national form; email shows first character and domain.
